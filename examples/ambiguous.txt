Make tracking secure and safe.

Analyze this request against docs/01-business-requirement.md,
docs/02-requirement-analysis.md, docs/03-engineering-design.md, and the current
codebase. Do not guess what “secure and safe” means. Identify the key questions
for a human to answer, including what tracking data is required, privacy and
retention expectations, access controls, abuse threats, and acceptable event
loss or delay.

The current design uses Redis Streams and an asynchronous worker for click
analytics, and does not require storing IP addresses or user-agent values.
Treat those as current design facts, not permission to collect additional
personal data. Do not propose replacing the analytics architecture unless
answers to the open questions justify it.

Return a structured draft containing an interpretation, assumptions, open
questions, risks, candidate task breakdown with dependencies, tests, and
acceptance checks. Separate confirmed requirements from options that need a
decision. Mark it pending human review.
